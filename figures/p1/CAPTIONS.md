**Figure P1-1. Detection and localization on the CryptoBench test split (n = 218 scored targets).** (a) Share of targets with a prediction at the site (pocket centre within 4 Å of a ligand atom; probability tools: any residue above threshold), Wilson 95% CI. (b) Mean residue-level MCC, bootstrap 95% CI (10,000 resamples). Filled: any-rank scoring; hollow: only the top-3 pockets may count (fpocket and P2Rank). The ranking of the two pocket tools reverses between the two rules (paired Wilcoxon, Holm-corrected, in the panel). (c) Mean AUPRC, bootstrap 95% CI; dashed line is the mean pocket-residue base rate (0.055). Thresholds: AE-PocketMiner 0.7, pLM-NN 0.75.

**Figure P1-2. Failure modes on the test split (n = 218).** Share of targets assigned each tag (whiskers: Wilson 95% CI). Tags are assigned in order miss → offset → fragmentation → over-spread → partial/success, with rules locked on the training folds before the test split was tagged (results/p1_tag_rules.json). Each tool has a characteristic failure: P2Rank misses, fpocket is offset, the probability tools over-spread.

**Figure P1-3. The lowest normal mode does no better than random motion (curated subset, n = 36 targets, 25 conformers per ladder).** Each small dot is one target: its value on the ladder along the lowest ANM mode (both directions, ±1.42 Å global Cα RMSD, no residue > 12 Å) minus its value on a control ladder along a random smooth motion at the same amplitudes. Large dot: mean difference, bar: bootstrap 95% CI. Right: targets higher on control : higher on NMA, and the paired Wilcoxon p. (a) Number of times site detection changes along the ordered ladder. (b) Headroom, the best conformer's MCC minus the apo's. The control gives more headroom for every structure tool, so best-of-25 gains are not evidence that the mode opens the pocket. pLM-NN is sequence-only, identical on every conformer, and omitted.

**Figure P1-4. The two openness axes disagree on KRAS G12D.** 25-conformer ladders from the prepared apo (vertical line). (a) Volume of the largest fpocket pocket within 8 Å of the ligand. (b) Cα RMSD of the true pocket residues to the holo after superposing on them (tool-independent). Along the normal mode the site volume rises from 282 Å³ (apo) to 746 Å³ (ladder position +1.18 Å, circled), while the pocket RMSD to holo goes from 1.66 to 1.97 Å: a larger fpocket volume is not a more holo-like pocket. The closest approach to holo is 1.65 Å along the normal mode and 1.56 Å along the random control.
