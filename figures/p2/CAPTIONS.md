# Phase 2 figures

**Figure 6. What predicts whether a target's prediction is good (n = 846 training targets).** AUC for separating each tool's well-predicted targets from the rest, by candidate confidence signal; bootstrap 95% CI, uncorrected for 15 comparisons. Filled: one global AUPRC median. Hollow: within base-rate quintiles, which removes the dependence of AUPRC on the base rate. Corrected, the probability tools' own scores reach 0.64 and 0.61, where the raw measurement put them near chance; fpocket's score and every perturbation signal stay there.

**Figure 7. Perturbation stability adds a little to a tool's own residue ranking.** Mean per-target gain in top-10 AUC over the tool's own apo score, by tool and motion generator; bootstrap 95% CI, with Holm-corrected paired Wilcoxon p at right (grey: p ≥ 0.05). (a) Stability alone. (b) Stability rank-summed with the tool's own score, so nothing is fitted. Random motion matches or beats the normal modes everywhere. fpocket gains most because its top-10 is a single tied block on 81% of targets. Hollow: held-out test split.

**Figure 8. Withholding unstable predictions buys precision only where the tool has no ranking of its own (n = 846 training targets).** Every target keeps the same share of its top-10 apo residues, ranked within the target. Annotated: gain of stability + own score over the own score alone at 20% coverage, paired cluster-bootstrap 95% CI. fpocket's own curve is flat on its base rate; stability supplies the missing ordering. P2Rank and AE-PocketMiner already rank their shortlists and gain nothing. Dashed: held-out test split.
